Phase 2b: bookings and revenue

Couples reserve dates with a deposit, and vendors start paying for plans. This is the first income from the marketplace.

Product
  • Deposits through PayHere (20% by default). The date gets blocked when PayHere confirms the payment
  • Reviews only from couples who booked, after the wedding date has passed
  • Free, Pro (Rs. 3,900/mo) and Premium (Rs. 9,900/mo) vendor plans
  • Featured listings, always clearly labelled
Operations
  • A written cancellation and refund policy
  • Weekly payouts to vendors, recorded in a payouts table
  • One person who handles disputes, and records every change in the audit log
Test to pass100 paid deposits with fewer than 2% disputed. 40 paying vendors. Rs. 0.9M monthly revenue.

What "perfectly built" means

These checks apply to every page before the Phase 3 launch, and to every PR after it. Most of them can run automatically in CI.

Speed

  • Page fully loads (LCP) in under 2.5 s on a mid-range Android over 4G
  • Responds to taps within 200 ms (INP); layout shifts under 0.1 (CLS)
  • Guest invitation page under 150 KB of JavaScript (gzipped)
  • Lighthouse score of 90+ for speed and 100 for SEO on key pages

Access and language

  • Meets the WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standard; buttons at least 44 px; works fully with a keyboard
  • Every word in si, ta and en, with lang set; Sinhala and Tamil text has no letter-spacing
  • No sideways scrolling at 360, 768 or 1280 px in any language

Security and privacy

  • Every query filtered by the signed-in owner, with tests proving one user can't see another's data
  • PayHere payment notices verified, and each one processed only once even if it arrives twice
  • Shared rate limits (Redis); guest links never logged
  • Collect as little personal data as possible; people can export or delete theirs; data deleted after the wedding

Reliability

  • 99.9% uptime; Sentry alerts sent to both owners
  • Database migrations tested on staging before going live
  • Daily backups, with a test restore every month
  • Playwright tests for the main paths: search, quote, booking, invitation, RSVP
